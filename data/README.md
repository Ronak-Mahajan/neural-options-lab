commit behind each shard are not stored in the files.

## Data fetched at run time and not committed

| used by | contents | source | window | time zone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `scripts/hedge_real_paths.py` | SPY and BTC-USD adjusted daily closes | Yahoo Finance through yfinance | the 8 years before the run date | SPY: New York session closes; BTC-USD: daily bars on UTC dates |
| `backend/quant/generative.py` | SPY daily history for the WGAN market simulator | Yahoo Finance through yfinance | 2000-01-01 to the run date | New York session closes |
| `backend/quant/market_data.py` | Spot, realised volatility and a Treasury rate for the dashboard's ticker loader | Yahoo Finance through yfinance | at request time | not stored |

Because the closes are fetched relative to the run date, a rerun of
`scripts/hedge_real_paths.py` covers a slightly different window from the committed output
in `docs/hedging_real_paths.txt`.

## Scheduled captures

`.github/workflows/record_surfaces.yml` keeps recording Deribit BTC and ETH chains, and SPY
chains during US trading hours, to the orphan `surfaces` branch. Those captures come from
the same sources and carry the same terms as the tables above.
