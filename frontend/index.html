What you are looking at

A small neural network has learned to price an exotic option (an arithmetic-average Asian option) directly from Monte Carlo simulations, so it answers in about a millisecond with the Greeks as exact derivatives. Move the sliders: the blue number is the network's price, the amber one is a fresh 50,000-path Monte Carlo run, and the middle card says whether they agree. At or below 12 trading days a second model trained on rough volatility takes over. The Deep Hedging tab shows a learned hedging policy losing to plain delta hedging, an honest negative result.
